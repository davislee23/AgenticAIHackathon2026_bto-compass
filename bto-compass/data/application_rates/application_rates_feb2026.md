## Reading these numbers

- **Rates are per applicant group.** Redhill Peaks 3-room is 0.7x for a first-timer family and
  17.7x for a second-timer family — same flats. Never average across groups.
- **Rates are a competition indicator, not a probability of winning.** Ballot chances vary by
  priority scheme and accumulated attempts. HDB advises applicants to consider projects with a
  rate of 1 or below.
- **You cannot pick a project within a town.** Sembawang and Tampines each had two projects
  sharing one ballot group; you select from what remains at your queue position.
- **Quotas apply.** For 4-room and larger flats, at least 90% of supply is set aside for
  first-timer families and up to 10% for second-timer families, which is why ST rates run so
  much higher on the same unit count.
