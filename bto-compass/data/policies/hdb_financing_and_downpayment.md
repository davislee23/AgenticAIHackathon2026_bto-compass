# Financing, Loans, and Downpayment Rules

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits
* **HDB Housing Loan:** The maximum LTV limit for HDB housing loans is **75%** of the flat purchase price. The remaining 25% can be paid using CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings, cash, or a combination of both.
* **Bank Loan (Financial Institutions):** Maximum LTV limit is **75%**, of which at least **5% must be paid in cash**.

---

## Interest Rates
* **HDB Housing Loan Interest Rate:** Pegged at **0.10% above the prevailing CPF Ordinary Account (OA) interest rate** (currently **2.60% per annum**).
* **Bank Loans:** Floating or fixed rates determined by the respective Financial Institution (FI).

---

## Staggered Downpayment Scheme
Allows eligible first-timer couples (young couples or students/NSFs) to split their downpayment into two separate payments.

* **First Tranche (Signing of Agreement for Lease):**
  * **HDB Loan:** Pay **5%** (via CPF OA or cash).
  * **Bank Loan:** Pay **5%** cash (or combination based on FI requirements).
* **Second Tranche (Key Collection):**
  * Balance downpayment paid upon collecting keys to the completed flat.
