# HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter Guidelines

## Overview & Purpose
* **Mandatory Requirement:** The HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter provides a holistic assessment of housing and financing options, confirming eligibility to buy a flat, receive CPF housing grants, and take up an HDB housing loan.
* **Resale Levy:** Second-time applicants will be informed of the resale levy or premium payable for purchasing a subsidised flat.
* **Household Consistency:** The applicant(s) and occupier(s) listed in the flat application must remain identical to those declared in the HFE letter.

---

## Integrated Loan Application Service (IPA)
* **In-Principle Approval (IPA):** Applicants can request an IPA from participating Financial Institutions (FIs) concurrently when applying for an HFE letter.
* **FI Outreach:** Chosen FIs reach out directly with loan indicative amounts and outcomes.
* **Confirmation:** Final loan offers from participating FIs are confirmed through the HDB Flat Portal after securing a flat purchase.

---

## Validity & Financial Review
* **Validity Period:** The HFE letter is valid for **9 months** from the date of issue.
* **Processing Expired Letters:** If a flat application was submitted while the HFE letter was valid, processing continues even if the HFE letter subsequently expires. Expired HFE letters not linked to a submitted application cannot be viewed.
* **Reasons for HFE Review:** An HFE letter outcome will be reviewed if:
  * Provided information is false or incorrect.
  * Terms and conditions for flat purchase, CPF grants, or HDB loans are breached.
  * Changes occur in household composition, personal circumstances, or HDB policies/processes.
* **Uncompleted Flats Re-assessment:** For uncompleted flats, HDB conducts a financial review nearer to completion. Adverse changes in servicing ability may lead to a reduced HDB housing loan limit.

---

## Household Details & Procedure Matrix

Changes to household details may invalidate an HFE letter and result in flat application cancellation (subject to standard financial forfeitures for new flats or legal liabilities under the OTP for resale flats).

| HFE Application Stage | Household Detail Change Type | Procedure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pending Approval** | Any household or personal detail change | Cancel the HFE application and submit a fresh HFE letter application (Steps 1 & 2) on the HDB Flat Portal. |
| **Approved** *(Before flat application)* | Change in household income only | **No action required.** |
| **Approved** *(Before flat application)* | Addition/removal of members, applicant/occupier role change, marital status, citizenship, or private property ownership | Cancel the current HFE letter and apply for a fresh HFE letter on the HDB Flat Portal before submitting a flat application. |
| **Approved** *(After flat application)* | Any structural household detail change | Submit a request via **MyRequest@HDB** including your HFE reference number and sales registration / resale case number. |

---

## Re-application Guidelines
* **Expiring HFE Letters:** If an HFE letter expires within 30 days and more time is needed to apply for a flat, submit a re-application via the HDB Flat Portal (*My Flat Dashboard > Applying an HDB Flat Eligibility letter > Step 2 > View/Re-apply HFE Letter*).
* **No Member Changes:** Re-application can be initiated by the first applicant if there are no changes to listed members other than updated employment or income.
* **Existing HFE Use:** Until a fresh HFE letter is granted, the existing valid HFE letter can still be used for flat purchases.

## New Guidelines(Manual Entry)
The gross monthly income ceiling for single Singapore Citizens buying a 2-room Flexi BTO flat is SGD 8,000.
