# CPF Housing Grants Framework

## Overview
* CPF Housing Grants are provided by the government to lower the cost of purchasing public housing for eligible first-timer Singapore Citizen (SC) buyers.
* Grants are disbursed into the eligible applicants' CPF Ordinary Account (OA) to offset the flat purchase price or downpayment (cannot be used for cash monthly mortgage payments).

---

## Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) (August 2024 Updates)
* **Maximum Grant Amount:** Up to **$120,000** for eligible first-timer families and up to **$60,000** for eligible first-timer singles.
* **Continuous Employment:** Must have worked continuously for at least 12 months prior to the HFE application and be currently employed.
* **Lease Requirement:** The flat must have sufficient lease to cover the youngest buyer/spouse up to 95 years of age; otherwise, the grant amount is pro-rated.
