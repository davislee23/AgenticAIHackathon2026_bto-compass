# Property Ownership, Wait-Out Periods, and Penalties

## Private Property Ownership & Wait-Out Period
* **Existing Private Property Owners:** Applicants who currently own or have a share in private residential property (local or overseas) are **ineligible** to apply for a BTO flat.
* **Wait-Out Period for BTO:** Former owners of private residential property must wait **30 months** from the date of disposing of the private property before they can apply for an HFE letter for a new BTO flat.

---

## Non-Selection Penalties for BTO Sales Exercises
Applicants who are invited to book a flat but choose not to do so will face non-selection penalties to ensure fair allocation to serious buyers.

* **First-Timer Applicants:** 
  * If a first-timer family passes up **1 opportunity** to book a flat, their first-timer priority status is revoked for **1 year** for subsequent BTO/SBF applications (treated as a second-timer).
* **Second-Timer Applicants:**
  * If a second-timer passes up **1 opportunity** to book a flat, they will be barred from participating in BTO/SBF sales exercises for **1 year**.

---

## Resale Levy Policy
First-timer owners who sell their subsidized HDB flat and subsequently purchase a second subsidized flat (e.g., a second BTO flat) must pay a resale levy.

* **2-Room Flat:** $15,000
* **3-Room Flat:** $30,000
* **4-Room Flat:** $40,000
* **5-Room Flat:** $45,000
* **Executive Flat:** $50,000
