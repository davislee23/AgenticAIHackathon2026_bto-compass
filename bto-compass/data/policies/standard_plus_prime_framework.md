# Standard, Plus, and Prime Housing Framework

## Overview
* The location-based classification of Build-To-Order (BTO) flats ensures the public housing system meets housing needs and aspirations.
* Effective from the **October 2024** sales exercise onwards, new BTO projects are classified as Standard, Plus, or Prime based on locational attributes.
* This new classification **does not apply** to existing HDB flats or flats launched before October 2024.

## Flat Classifications
* **Standard Flats:** Form the largest category of BTO flats launched every year and come with significant market discounts.
* **Plus Flats:** Located in choicer locations across Singapore with good connectivity and proximity to amenities or the city centre (e.g., waterfront living).
* **Prime Flats:** Sited in the choicest locations, typically centrally located with comprehensive amenities and excellent transport. Includes Prime Location Public Housing (PLH) sold before October 2024.
* **Pricing & Affordability:** Due to attractive attributes, Plus and Prime flats naturally command higher market values. They are priced with additional subsidies to remain affordable across income levels and carry tighter resale and rental conditions to ensure owner-occupation.

## Key Features Comparison

| Feature | Standard | Plus | Prime |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subsidies** | Significant market discounts | More subsidies on top of market discounts | Most subsidies on top of market discounts |
| **Subsidy Recovery (Resale)*** | No | Yes (less than Prime) | Yes |
| **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP)**| 5 years | 10 years | 10 years |
| **Resale of Flat*** | Allowed after MOP | Allowed after MOP (tighter conditions) | Allowed after MOP (tighter conditions) |
| **Invest in Private Property** | Allowed after MOP | Allowed after MOP | Allowed after MOP |
| **Renting Whole Flat*** | Allowed after MOP | Not allowed | Not allowed |
| **Renting Spare Rooms** | Allowed for 3-room or bigger | Allowed for 3-room or bigger | Allowed for 3-room or bigger |

*\* 2-room Flexi flats sold on short-lease and Community Care Apartments cannot be rented out (bedroom or whole flat) or sold on the open market.*

## Subsidy Recovery Conditions
* **Buying directly from HDB:** Upon selling or transferring a Plus or Prime flat bought from HDB, you must return a percentage of the resale price (or valuation, whichever is higher) to HDB. 
* **Recovery Rate:** The percentage is commensurate with the extra subsidies provided to ensure fairness, and is made known when the projects are launched for sale.
* **Resale Market Purchases:** If you buy a resale Plus or Prime flat on the open market, the subsidy recovery **does not apply** when you subsequently sell it. However, the 10-year MOP, whole-flat rental ban, and tighter buyer eligibility conditions still apply.

## More Housing Options for Singles
Starting from the October 2024 sales exercise, eligible singles have expanded housing options.

| Flat Type | Classification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **New 2-room Flexi flat** | Standard, Plus, and Prime |
| **Resale 2-room flat** | Prime |
| **Resale flat (all types except 3Gen)**| Standard, Plus, and existing unclassified flats |

## Eligibility Conditions
* **Valid HFE Letter:** All applicants must have a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter to apply for a Standard, Plus, or Prime flat in a sales exercise.
* **Timeline:** Apply for the HFE letter at least 1 month ahead of the sales exercise to plan financing and familiarize yourself with buying procedures.
* **In-Principle Approval:** The HFE letter provides a holistic understanding of housing options, CPF grants, and HDB loan limits. Buyers can concurrently apply for an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter for a bank loan from participating financial institutions.
