# CPF Housing Grants for Resale Flats [RESALE ONLY]

## Overview of Resale Grants
Unlike BTO (which only offers the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant), buyers of HDB Resale flats can stack up to **3 separate CPF housing grants**:

1. **Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG):** Up to $120,000 (Families) or $60,000 (Singles), based on income tiers.
2. **CPF Housing Grant for Resale (Family Grant / Single Grant):**
   * **First-Timer Families:** $80,000 (2-Room to 4-Room) or $50,000 (5-Room or larger).
   * **First-Timer Singles (Aged 35+):** $40,000 (2-Room to 4-Room) or $25,000 (5-Room).
3. **Proximity Housing Grant (PHG):**
   * **Living with Parents/Children:** $30,000 (Families) or $15,000 (Singles).
   * **Living within 4km of Parents/Children:** $20,000 (Families) or $10,000 (Singles).

## Maximum Total Grant Caps for Resale
* **First-Timer Families:** Up to **$230,000** ($120k EHG + $80k Family Grant + $30k PHG).
* **First-Timer Singles:** Up to **$115,000** ($60k EHG + $40k Single Grant + $15k PHG).
