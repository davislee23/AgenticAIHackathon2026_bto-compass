# HDB Priority Schemes

## Overview
* **Purpose:** Priority schemes enhance your chances in the computer ballot for flats offered in the Build-To-Order (BTO) and Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercises. 
* **Application Limit:** You may apply for **1 priority scheme** if your household meets the eligibility conditions when applying for your HFE letter.
* **Exception (FPPS):** If you qualify for the Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS), you may apply for **1 additional priority scheme**, such as the Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity) or Third Child Priority Scheme (TCPS).
* **Disclaimer:** Priority schemes improve ballot chances but do not guarantee a favourable queue number for flat selection.

---

## Priority for First-Timer Families
First-timer families enjoy greater priority and privileges when applying for a flat. 
* **Mixed Couples:** Couples comprising a first-timer and a second-timer are treated as first-timer families and enjoy the same priority benefits.

**Privileges include:**
* **Higher Flat Supply:** A significantly higher proportion of flat supply is set aside for first-timer families.
* **Priority Schemes:** Access to specific schemes to enhance ballot chances.
* **Additional Ballot Chances:** First-timers receive extra ballot chances during BTO or SBF exercises compared to second-timers.

---

## First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples) / FT(PMC)
Among first-timer families, those with young children and young married couples buying their first home may qualify for the **FT(PMC)** category.
* **Support:** Additional support is provided for applicants in this category to help young families settle down more quickly.

---

## Directory of Priority Schemes

**1. Family and Parenthood Priority Scheme (FPPS)**
* Helps first-timer married couples with child(ren) and young married couples to get their flat more easily.

**2. Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Proximity)**
* Helps parents and their child(ren), married or single, to live with or near each other for mutual care and support.

**3. Family Care Scheme (FCS) (Joint Balloting)**
* Allows parents and their child(ren), married or single, to make a joint application for 2 units in a BTO project that offers 2-room Flexi or 3-room flats.

**4. Third Child Priority Scheme (TCPS)**
* Helps families with 3 or more children improve their ballot chances.

**5. Assistance Scheme for Second-Timers (Divorced/ Widowed Parents) (ASSIST)**
* Supports divorced or widowed persons with children in securing a home.

**6. Senior Priority Scheme (SPS) (Near Existing Home)**
* Helps seniors who wish to buy a 2-room Flexi flat to age-in-place in a familiar environment.

**7. Tenants’ Priority Scheme (TPS)**
* Helps current tenants of HDB public rental flats to purchase their own home.
